# KhelSpot brand brief

Built with the brand-building skills (brand-positioning, brand-messaging, brand-voice, brand-identity), working from `.agents/brand-context.md` and the Assignment #1 proposal. Logo canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/3esBZTKekF1yMA175Czrdr

## 1. Positioning

**Stated category:** sports venue booking app.
**Actual category (what players compare us to):** WhatsApp messages and phone calls to venues.
**Category to own:** instant court booking for every indoor sport, not only padel.

**Competitive map** (Axis 1: player booking only ↔ full venue software; Axis 2: one sport/one city ↔ all indoor sports/many cities)
- Turfy: venue software, Lahore. Strong owner tools, no online payment yet, Pro at PKR 4,500/month.
- Now V Play: player app plus e-gaming, gyms, academies, Lahore. Discount-led, unfocused.
- PlayPro: padel community, 7 cities. Refund complaints, app crashes.
- WhatsApp/phone: the real default. Free, slow, cash.
- **Open space:** confirmed booking with online payment across padel, futsal, indoor cricket and badminton, at a price small venues can pay.

**Positioning statement:** For players in Pakistan's cities who want a court tonight, KhelSpot is the booking service that shows free slots and real prices for every indoor sport and confirms the booking when you pay. The reason to believe: payment, refund rules and confirmation are built in from day one, and venues pay Rs 999 a month instead of thousands.

**Conversational version:** See which courts are free near you, what they cost, and book one without sending a single WhatsApp.

**Proof points**
1. JazzCash, Easypaisa and card payment at launch (competitors: cash, bank transfer, or "coming soon").
2. Cancellation and refund rules shown before you pay (top PlayPro complaint).
3. City and sport filters (requested in competitor reviews).
4. Automatic WhatsApp/SMS confirmation.
5. Owner plan Rs 999/month after a free month, about 78% below PKR 4,500.

**What KhelSpot refuses to be**
- Not a discount marketplace. We do not compete on 50%-off deals.
- Not a padel-only club. Futsal, cricket and badminton get equal space.
- Not a gaming, gym or academy app.

**One sentence:** KhelSpot lets you book any indoor court in your city in a minute, with the price and refund rules shown upfront.

## 2. Messaging

**Core message:** KhelSpot helps players book indoor courts without the WhatsApp back-and-forth by putting free slots, prices and payment in one place.

**Taglines**
| Tagline | Style | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Book the court, not the call.** | Functional/witty | Names the exact pain. **Recommended.** |
| Your slot, confirmed. | Functional | Plain, good for confirmation screens. |
| Khelo jab dil kare. | Emotional, Urdu | Good for social; weaker on the problem. |
| Every court in the city, one app. | Functional | Accurate, flat. |

**Hierarchy**
- **Headline:** Book the court, not the call.
- **Supporting:** See free slots and prices at padel, futsal, indoor cricket and badminton courts near you. Pay with JazzCash, Easypaisa or card and get the confirmation on WhatsApp.
- **Key messages**
  1. *See what's free before you ask.* Live slots and per-hour prices, by city and sport. Proof: city and sport filters.
  2. *Paid means booked.* The slot is yours once payment goes through. Proof: online payment from day one.
  3. *Refund rules up front.* You see the cancellation window before you pay. Proof: rules shown at checkout.
  4. *Every indoor sport.* Padel, futsal, indoor cricket, badminton. Proof: launch categories.
- **Owner message:** Stop running bookings from your phone. Calendar, online payments, customer records and revenue in one dashboard for Rs 999 a month, first month free.

**Do not say:** "seamless", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "best in Pakistan", user counts or ratings we don't have, invented testimonials.

## 3. Voice

KhelSpot sounds like the friend in the group chat who already booked the court: quick, plain-spoken, and specific about times and prices.

- Casual, not sloppy. Contractions yes.
- Short sentences. Numbers as numerals (9 PM, Rs 4,000).
- Specific over clever: a time, a price, a sport.
- A little Urdu where players use it (khel, slot, rate), never forced.
- Calm about money: refunds and payments are stated plainly, no jokes.
- Exclamation marks: almost never. Em dashes: avoid.

**Words we use:** slot, court, ground, book, free, confirmed, rate, tonight, near you, refund.
**Words we avoid:** seamless, effortless, elevate, unlock, revolutionize, ecosystem, world-class, hassle-free, one-stop solution.

Before: "Experience seamless, hassle-free sports booking with our revolutionary platform!"
After: "See which courts are free at 9 PM and book one in a minute."

## 4. Visual identity

**Strategy:** A floodlit court at night is the brand's picture: dark pitch, white lines, one bright light. The identity uses a dark ground with one sharp accent, sports-signage type, and real data (times, prices) as decoration.

**Logo:** symbol + wordmark. The symbol is a map pin whose head is a court seen from above, with the net down the middle and a ball in one half. Pin = Spot, court = Khel. Wordmark set in Archivo Expanded ExtraBold, "Spot" in turf green (floodlight yellow on dark). Avoid: balls with motion streaks, trophies, generic play-button arrows, gradients.

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Text / dark ground | Night pitch | `#0F1F18` |
| Primary | Turf | `#17804F` |
| Light ground | Chalk | `#F6F3EA` |
| Accent (on dark, or as fill under dark text) | Floodlight | `#FFD43B` |

Rule: never put Floodlight text on Chalk (fails contrast). Turf on Chalk passes for text at 4.5:1.

**Type:** Archivo (width 125, weight 800) for headlines; Instrument Sans for body; JetBrains Mono for slot times, prices and data labels.

**Imagery:** night games under lights, real courts in Lahore and Karachi, groups of friends. No stock handshakes, no glossy studio shots.

**Icons:** 2px stroke line icons, rounded joins.

**Design principles**
1. *Show the slot.* Times and prices appear in the design itself.
2. *One light.* One accent per view, like a single floodlight.
3. *Plain before clever.* If a label could be misread, rewrite it.

# Brand Context

Source: E-Commerce Assignment #1 project proposal (KhelSpot), Fall 2026.

## Brand
- **Name**: KhelSpot ("khel" = play/sport in Urdu, "spot" = a place, and a free slot)
- **Category**: Technology / sports venue booking service
- **Description**: Find an indoor court or ground near you, see the free slots and the price, and book and pay in one go.
- **Stage**: Pre-launch
- **Website**: N/A

## Audience
- **Primary Audience (players)**: Young adults and working groups in Pakistani cities (Lahore and Karachi first) who play padel, futsal, indoor cricket and badminton, usually at night and in groups.
- **Secondary Audience (venue owners)**: Small and mid-size indoor venues that run ads but still take bookings by WhatsApp or phone, often cash only.
- **Key Problem**: To book a court you message or call several venues, wait for replies about timings and rates, and often pay cash at the counter. Owners spend money on ads, then handle every booking by hand.
- **Their Language**: "padel near me", "indoor cricket ground near me", "badminton court near me with fees", "slot available?", "rate kya hai?", "for booking check bio", "send WhatsApp message", "night match", "Ramadan night cricket".

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Instant confirmed booking with online payment (JazzCash / Easypaisa / card) from day one; cancellation and refund rules shown before you pay; filters by city and sport; automatic WhatsApp/SMS confirmations; covers all indoor sports, not just padel; owner plan at Rs 999/month (about 78% below Turfy's Pro plan at PKR 4,500).
- **Competitors**: Turfy (venue OS, Lahore, Rs 0 limited / PKR 4,500 Pro, no online payments yet), Now V Play (multi-sport plus e-gaming, Lahore, discount-led), PlayPro (padel community, 7 cities, refund complaints and app bugs). Also Playra, SportsNow, Poncho, Playtomic, Padel Club PK, On Spot.
- **Market Position**: Value: affordable and dependable, booking first.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: straightforward, quick, local, fair, game-ready
- **Tone**: Casual and direct. Lightly playful, never jokey about money or refunds.
- **Voice Admires**: none named

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Clear prices, a booking that actually holds, fair refunds, every sport counts
- **Mission**: Make booking an indoor court in Pakistan as quick as ordering food, and give small venues software they can afford.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Sign up the first venues (owner free trial) and build a waitlist of players in Lahore and Karachi before launch.
- **Key Metrics**: Venue trial sign-ups, paid venues at Rs 999/month, player waitlist sign-ups, bookings per venue.

## Demand evidence (from the proposal, for proof points)
- Google Trends (Pakistan): padel rose from 0–5 (2021–mid 2023) to a peak of 100 in March 2026; still 55–67 in Sept–Oct 2026, more than 10x its 2023 level.
- Indoor cricket spikes every Feb–March (Ramadan night-cricket season).
- Breakout queries: "padel court", "padel near me", "indoor cricket ground near me", "badminton court near me with fees"; "badminton court near me" +400%, "sports complex near me" +300%.
- Keyword Planner: "padel court", "padel courts near me", "badminton court near me" each 1K–10K searches/month, low competition, top-of-page bids under PKR 300.
- Facebook Ads Library: ~83 active ads for "padel court booking", ~16 for "indoor cricket". Almost every one sends people to WhatsApp or a phone call.
- Unit economics: Rs 999/month price, ~Rs 200 cost per venue, ~80% margin.
